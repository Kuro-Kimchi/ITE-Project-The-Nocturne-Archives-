Fan-made reading sample · Fantasy / Strategy / Comedy

Synopsis

A man wakes in a fantasy world as a minor noble known for poor decisions. Determined to live comfortably, he discovers that careful preparation and unexpected loyalty can pull him into larger events.

Chapter 1: The Noble’s Plan for a Quiet Life

Cale’s first plan was simple: avoid trouble, keep the estate comfortable, and never volunteer for anything heroic. He wrote the rules in a notebook and placed it beside a plate of pastries as if the world could be negotiated with a checklist.

The plan lasted until a village messenger arrived with news of a flood-damaged road. Cale sent supplies, hired workers, and asked the local engineer for a safer route. He insisted that every decision was merely practical, although he stayed awake until the messenger returned with proof that the villagers were safe.

By morning, his quiet schedule had become a list of new responsibilities. Cale stared at it, sighed, and added a rule: help people before problems become disasters. He would deny that it was kindness if anyone asked, but he kept the villagers’ thank-you note in the same notebook as his plans.

This fan-made sample is an original piece created for a student website project; it is not an official chapter of Trash of the Count’s Family.
